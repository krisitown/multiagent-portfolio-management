# Funding, margin and faithful role handoffs

## Distinct resource claims

Gross strike funding for standard physically delivered short puts is the sum of absolute short quantities × applicable shares per contract × strike, assuming all those puts are assigned. A long put is not a short-put purchase obligation. Gross totals across dates do not by themselves represent a simultaneous event. Compatible offsets, already-held shares, exercise choices, cash settlement and adjusted deliverables can change the relevant cash/exposure analysis; they require correct terms and scenario timing. Gross strike funding is still a useful explicitly scoped number without being an account-capacity verdict.

Margin is collateral required under broker and regulatory arrangements. It is distinct from gross settlement funding, economic maximum loss, cash and buying power. For the IBKR securities-account definitions cited in [M7], available funds concern equity with loan value less initial margin and excess liquidity concerns equity with loan value less maintenance margin; displayed look-ahead and overnight measures answer different timing questions. Other account segments or brokers can differ. A field name without its applicable definition is insufficient to transplant the formula.

Do not subtract gross strike funding from excess liquidity and call the result remaining margin. Cash available for settlement, collateral eligibility/haircuts, currency financing, settlement dates and broker recognition matter. A loan increases an asset and an obligation; reporting-currency translation does not create resources. A snapshot can establish dated account values, not a guaranteed post-trade or post-assignment margin cushion. Missing current capacity supports uncertainty in the dependent capacity claim, not either guaranteed safety or an automatic liquidation conclusion.

A cash shortfall relative to a purchase is a funding gap on the identified resources; by itself it neither proves nor contradicts compliance with broker margin requirements. Authorized borrowing against eligible collateral may fund a purchase, but permission to borrow is not proof of sufficient capacity. Conversely, cash sufficient for that purchase, or hypothetical additional resources covering its gap, does not establish compliance across the account's other positions and liabilities. Post-event compliance depends on the applicable account/segment, equity and collateral, initial and maintenance requirements, and broker house rules; a relevant broker preview or equivalent supported account calculation can address that question within its time and scenario scope [M7, M11]. Unknown compliance should remain unknown in the explanation as well as its final qualification.

An assignment exchanges cash/financing for the delivered asset under the contract; its gross payment is not automatically a same-size reduction in equity. The asset's market value, prior option liability, price moves and fees matter to equity, while maintenance requirements can change with the resulting portfolio. Broker expiry exposure controls can also apply even where a terminal spread payoff looks limited [M8]. A theoretical payoff hedge does not establish automatic exercise coordination or broker margin offset.

Return percentages need an explicit denominator and horizon. Return on gross strike funding, net debit, equity at risk and broker margin are different quantities. Annualizing a short period does not establish a repeatable yield; it embeds an extrapolation. A high premium or high return on a small initial margin does not prove favorable risk/reward or affordable scale. An unsupported sizing assertion is factual/conditional overreach; choosing among valid risk preferences remains outside the checker's mandate.

## Public research and private context

Public Analysts do not need private holdings to analyze a public issuer claim, quote or hypothetical spread. The checker must not treat their absence as a research failure. A private Expert check can reconcile selected original signed holdings and owner statements supplied for that call, but cannot independently authenticate them or invent private intent from a ticker or position. An owner may call a position speculative without claiming a sector thesis, ownership target or permanent holding preference. The supplied owner statement supports its attributed view, not the truth of the market thesis.

A hard restriction, soft preference, historical account fact and hypothesis have different roles. Fact checking can expose a false attribution or a claim that an unconfigured limit was passed. It cannot impose an unwritten risk tolerance, cash-only rule, target allocation, required candidate count or favored strategy. Material conflict between supplied instructions should be identified accurately; it must not be resolved by fabricating owner consent.

## Synthesis and faithful forwarding

The Expert's intact final assessment is the substantive original. A Lead can report operational coverage and transport status without composing a competing investment recommendation. Exact original Expert forwarding remains required, with its stated limitations intact. It is a transport property, not a semantic checker approval or an independent verification of external facts. Lead, Explorer and Hermes have no checker-call requirement in this design.

An Analyst or Expert may summarize evidence or their own analysis. Compression becomes a material problem when it changes numbers, reverses a preferred course, hides a decisive condition, converts a conditional alternative to an instruction, removes an unresolved dependency, or turns tentative evidence into established fact. “Consider X if the quote is confirmed” is not equivalent to “Do X now.” A summary can be shorter without reproducing every section. Accept faithful paraphrases and harmless reordering when the submitted material supports them. These consistency principles do not create a separate checker requirement for downstream summaries.

The checker returns ordinary feedback to the requesting author; it does not edit the Expert text, send a message, publish a report or grant an agent email privileges. If a submitted synthesis and its selected source conflict, identify the faulty passage and its supplied reference. If a source premise itself remains uncertain, distinguish that uncertainty from a misrepresentation introduced by synthesis. Later delivery must not imply that a summary or source received independent verification merely because an earlier author used the checker.

## Bounded review and honest limits

A check can find the reasoning supported within supplied evidence and assumptions without establishing portfolio suitability, external truth or forecast success. Absence of an identified error does not prove a model infallible. A fresh source, revised draft or changed assumptions can require a new explicit call by the author. The maximum three assessments covers initial calls and author-requested rechecks; it does not entitle the author to three full rounds if shared budgets end sooner. Every check is fresh and has no conversation history.

When a limitation blocks meaningful assessment of an asserted material claim, it must remain visible in the author's dependent conclusion. The author repairs, qualifies or removes unsupported claims and explicitly seeks another check as appropriate within the limits. No automatic checker invocation, host approval/hash gate, semantic JSON approval parser or forced repair loop exists. Tool failure, an unreadable response or exhausted budget supplies no confirmation. Neither checker nor author may conceal unresolved support in optimistic prose or a generic risk disclaimer. A valid conditional forecast, preference or qualified non-answer does not need to become certain to be useful.
