## M1 — The Options Industry Council (OCC)
[Bear Put Spread](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/bear-put-spread)

Inspected: Description, Example, Max Loss, Max Gain, Profit/Loss and Breakeven; retrieved lines 163-215.
Support: Long higher-strike put and short lower-strike put share an expiration. Maximum profit is width less debit; maximum loss is debit; expiration breakeven is long strike less debit.
Limits: Expiration strategy economics do not determine a pre-expiry fill or guarantee matched settlement.

## M2 — The Options Industry Council (OCC)
[Bull Call Spread (Debit Call Spread)](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/bull-call-spread-debit-call-spread)

Inspected: Description, Example, Max Loss, Max Gain, Profit/Loss, Breakeven and Assignment Risk; retrieved lines 163-224.
Support: Long lower-strike call and short higher-strike call share an expiration. Maximum profit is width less debit; maximum loss is debit; expiration breakeven is long strike plus debit.
Limits: Theoretical expiration bounds omit transaction costs and operational consequences of assignment.

## M3 — The Options Industry Council (OCC)
[Options Assignment](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/options-assignment)

Inspected: Assignment timing/allocation, closing shorts, exercise-by-exception and holder discretion; retrieved FAQ body lines 80-123.
Support: American-style shorts can be assigned early; clearing-member and customer allocation are separate steps. The $0.01 exercise-by-exception threshold is not a guarantee; contrary instructions and out-of-the-money exercise are possible. Put assignment creates an obligation to buy at the strike.
Limits: No calibrated probability for an individual short position; aggregate exercise frequency is not its assignment probability.

## M4 — The Options Industry Council (OCC)
[Options Delta](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/delta)

Inspected: Delta definition, signs, changing inputs and probability interpretation; retrieved lines 76-123.
Support: Delta describes modeled premium sensitivity. Long puts have negative per-unit delta; signed shorts reverse sensitivity. Some traders use delta as an expiration-in-the-money proxy; it changes with inputs.
Limits: This educational proxy is not a calibrated assignment or profit probability; the page supplies no such model.

## M7 — Interactive Brokers — Trader Workstation User Guide
