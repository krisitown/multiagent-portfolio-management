# Investment questions and responsibilities

PM1 coordination reference, 2026-11-02. This is knowledge about role ownership and evidence boundaries, not a screening recipe, mandatory question set, reasoning sequence or report template. The active control and actual runtime contracts govern authority.

## Responsibility map

| Responsibility | Owner | Scope and limitations |
|---|---|---|

| Access, immutable records, request accounting and parent-review lineage | Runtime | Exposed capabilities only; an identifier is not permission, and a claimed history is not verified lineage. |

| Presentation and authorized external delivery | Hermes/host | Lead delivery cannot add, repair or replace investment conclusions; operational notes are separate from the original assessment. |

A request for a public fact is not authority for an Analyst to judge the private account. Additional research can reach the same Expert invocation without becoming a new initial agenda or another assessment. Actual exposed tools determine retrieval access. Original forwarding, citations and ordinary completion do not certify financial correctness.

## Public evidence and private judgment

Business quality and entry terms are related but distinct. Public company evidence may support an ownership case without establishing an attractive price; quoted premium does not establish business quality. Their significance for the particular owner belongs to Expert judgment. No factual research question authorizes the Lead to rank the trade.

Contract identity, quote basis, units and dates determine what an observation describes. Current cash flow, historical book results and funding are different quantities. Public exact-contract observations can inform private decisions without revealing quantity, ownership or basis. A request that substitutes private book loss for closing cash changes both the privacy boundary and the economic question.

An event calendar describes chronology, not necessarily resulting portfolio exposure. Expiry, exercise and assignment can leave different holdings and obligations. Public contract/event evidence has an estimated or confirmed status independent of the owner's position. Earlier expiry alone does not establish absence of later risk; that inference belongs neither in a neutral assignment nor in a Lead investment conclusion.

Portfolio fit, concentration, hedge allocation, capacity and management priorities depend on private context and substantive judgment. Public evidence can be relevant without revealing why the owner needs it. A funding preference is not a verified borrowing limit, and a dated capacity observation is not a current guarantee. A missing field establishes neither a financial verdict nor a universal analysis block.

A retained source passage or observed value provides a different basis from a citation alone. Subject, period, date, conditions and attribution limit the claim. Several summaries can repeat one source rather than corroborate it independently. An unresolved source dependency limits the affected claim while unrelated supported findings can remain useful. A Lead-written reconciliation is not a substitute for original conflicting evidence.

## Review scope and time

A full report without fresh opportunity research or relevant existing-position coverage is incomplete, including when no change is ultimately justified. Neither breadth nor completion requires a fixed number of names. Supported negative, conditional or hold judgments are different from failure to obtain the requested research.

A parent-linked `explanation` is confined to retained dated evidence. `targeted` and `refresh` follow-ups permit material new observations within their stated scope, not an unrequested full pipeline. A question received today does not make its inherited sources current. Original and new observations, questions and portfolio revisions have distinct identities and applicability.

The host's supplied parent-review lineage establishes what was retained. No bare run identifier grants memory or private-source access. An old Expert conclusion cannot be represented as having evaluated a later change. No hidden reasoning or reconstructed conversation is needed for an attributed public assessment and its dated evidence to remain useful.

These are project design decisions, not externally established financial facts or evidence of runtime quality.

No current company observations, prices, fixed trading thresholds or return forecasts are asserted here. Financial claims added later need appropriate source support and applicability limits; routing knowledge does not substitute for the Expert's substantive library.
