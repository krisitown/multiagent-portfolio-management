# Practical coordination reference

PM1 coordination reference, 2026-11-02. This describes relationships among questions, evidence, scope and authority. It does not prescribe research steps, a financial checklist, a preferred hypothesis or an output format. The active request, exposed tools and runtime limits define permitted action.

## Questions and authority

A public assignment can express an unresolved fact or comparison without predicting its answer. Hypotheses, sources, depth and interpretations belong to the Analyst's investigation. Prior reports are evidence to examine, not instructions to agree. A request for source support is not authority for an exhaustive dump or identical depth across unrelated questions.

A task's coherence depends on its question, not its place in a candidate list. Splitting one problem can create dependencies or duplicate acquisition; parallelism alone does not improve coverage. Discovery can produce promising leads or meaningful exclusions without conferring Expert endorsement. A favorable candidate is not a valid mandatory outcome of an investigation. Negative, partial and unresolved answers can carry useful evidence.

The Lead's authority concerns work and resources. It does not extend to what the owner should trade. Recognition of an apparent error or source conflict does not confer authority to repair the Expert's conclusion, dictate an Analyst's answer or suppress an inconvenient original result.

## Dependencies, context and handoff

A real dependency exists when one result is necessary to interpret or perform another task. List order alone is not a dependency. Public investigation related to existing instruments and opportunity discovery can be independent. A full report lacks required scope without fresh Explorer research, but that does not impose discovery-first ordering. Concurrency is a capacity limit, not a candidate count or coverage guarantee.

Permitted public evidence can be reusable without new acquisition. A job ID alone is neither report content nor source access. A public reference cannot authorize access to the private portfolio, owner context or private calculations. Reused findings retain their original scope, dates and qualifications; repetition adds no independent corroboration.

The single no-argument `consult_expert()` boundary follows the initial work appropriate to the request, including its actual failure or limit. The host carries original research and supplied private context. There is no Lead selector, reconstructed account, financial retelling or custom Expert question in that handoff. The initial pass need not anticipate every fact the Expert may find consequential; in an explanation follow-up, dated retained context creates no obligation for fresh research.

The Expert's own exposed `request_research` tool connects to the host Analyst scheduler during the same Expert invocation. Original outcomes return directly to that conversation while the Lead awaits the consultation. No Lead-model reentry, recursive Analyst delegation or second Expert is part of the mechanism. Duplicate unchanged requests can map to an existing original outcome. Reuse is neither fresh evidence nor a reset of resources; requested and original job identities need not be identical when reuse is reported.

## Meaning, support and confidentiality

Observations, source assertions, calculations and interpretations have different support. Instrument, units, dates, subject and conditions determine what a public finding means. A current fetch can contain an old observation. Missing evidence does not establish that an alternative is unsuitable, and a conditional source statement does not establish a confirmed result.

A source citation alone does not prove inspection. Original values or concise passages can support a comparison that a summary alone cannot. Conflicting observations remain different observations unless the responsible role resolves their identities and applicability. A Lead-created average, stronger paraphrase or omitted qualification is not independent verification.

Hermes-supplied owner context is distinct from holdings and public research. An attributed preference or hypothesis can be relevant privately without becoming a verified market fact, hard constraint or public instruction. The reason a public fact matters to a held position can itself be private. Public researchers have no authority to receive that rationale or the holdings-to-job mapping.

Capability isolation restricts private-reference access; it cannot undo disclosure inside free-form prose. Exact knowledge of a private ID is not permission, and successful tool execution does not certify confidentiality. No private account or owner-context reconstruction belongs in a public assignment.

## Limits and incomplete work

A completed tool call can leave its question unanswered. Source unavailability, partial research, failed invocation, denied access and substantive disagreement are distinct outcomes; none may be relabeled to conceal a limitation. Failure of one task does not erase successful neighboring evidence.

The host's deadline, generations, concurrency and request allowances govern the run. They are shared ceilings rather than targets or new per-child budgets. No job renaming, recursive workflow, timeout extension or replacement Expert may evade them. No retry of unchanged denied work or repeated broad research for uniformity is authorized by unused capacity. A limit neither supplies missing evidence nor justifies an invented conclusion.

Expert-requested work shares those same constraints. The Expert's final return ends the assessment. A missing answer, model failure or delivery problem does not authorize the Lead to reopen research, consult another Expert or provide its own portfolio recommendation.

## Follow-ups and changed inputs

An `explanation` can refer to the saved assessment and dated evidence without new discovery, research or market retrieval. A `targeted` or `refresh` follow-up permits scoped new observations alongside the supplied parent context. It is not implicit authority for fresh portfolio acquisition or daily discovery. A full rerun is a distinct requested scope.

New evidence can affect one dependent conclusion without invalidating every original finding. Original observations do not become current through reuse; new observations have their own identities and dates. A revised portfolio is not an amendment the prior Expert already considered. Parent-review lineage and supplied artifact context establish continuity, not invented durable memory or a hidden conversation transcript.

Material changes after final assessment are unreviewed by that assessment. Do not attach old approval to new terms, rewrite original evidence or relabel an old report current. The host owns intact portfolio transport; a transport mismatch is not permission for Lead reconstruction of account facts.

## Delivery and coverage claims

The substantive deliverable is the original Expert assessment. A necessary operational note is separate and limited to actual coverage, failed outcomes or runtime/transport limits. It cannot correct, strengthen, selectively summarize or replace financial judgment. Exact forwarding, ordinary completion and financial correctness are different properties.

Later original research may answer an earlier gap. An obsolete gap is not proof of current incompleteness, while an unexposed outcome cannot support a claim of verification. A partial assessment does not become complete through delivery. The host owns any separately authorized action-first presentation and external communication.

## Contrasting coordination examples

These synthetic examples explain distinctions. They are not required assignment wording, acceptance tests, trades or a prescribed sequence.

A private book loss and an unknown closing quote describe different quantities. Sending the book loss to an Analyst as the amount to fund reveals private information and assumes an economic interpretation. A public exact-contract observation has a different scope from the original private account available to the Expert. Unavailable current quotes can limit a price comparison without erasing known historical information.

An option expiring before a public event does not establish whether resulting holdings remain exposed at the event. Public contract terms and chronology, including estimated versus confirmed dates, are distinct from private conditional exposure. The Lead has no authority to disclose the position or insert a preferred management outcome into the question.

A source conditionally discussing production growth does not establish a confirmed cash-flow forecast. Subject, horizon, conditions and provenance determine what it supports; several URLs may repeat one assertion. The Expert can request a material public clarification through its host-backed research capability without a Lead roundtrip. The Lead is not authorized to replace the resulting assessment with a preferred forecast.
