# 04 — Sector differences and business-specific risks

DRAFT knowledge. A sector is a starting description, not a uniform risk category. The distinctions below help select evidence. They do not prescribe sector weights or imply that an entire sector qualifies or fails.

## Banks and other financial institutions

For a bank, deposits, lending, securities, credit losses, capital and liquidity are central to operations. Borrowing and financial assets cannot be interpreted as if they belonged to an industrial manufacturer. Assess the nature of funding, concentration of depositors and borrowers, asset quality, loss recognition, interest-rate sensitivity and regulatory capital using applicable disclosures. Public regulatory ratios have defined scopes; do not substitute book equity for a regulatory-capital measure. CFA's financial-institution reading explicitly treats banks and insurers differently from nonfinancial companies. [S14](../sources.md#s14)

A useful inquiry distinguishes solvency, earnings and liquidity. Assets can have positive long-run economic value while their sale at stressed market prices would crystallize losses. Deposit behavior and access to funding can therefore matter independently of an annual profit estimate. A low price-to-book ratio may signal opportunity, or it may reflect doubts about asset values and sustainable returns. Neither interpretation follows from the ratio alone.

Do not generalize from one bank to an exchange, asset manager, broker or payment network. Fee businesses may have different balance-sheet needs, operating leverage and counterparty exposures. A fintech label says little about whether the issuer is primarily a lender, software provider, intermediary or regulated depository institution.

## Insurance

Insurance analysis depends on underwriting discipline, reserving, investment assets, capital and the timing of claims. For property and casualty insurers, combined ratios and reserve development can inform underwriting performance, but definitions and business mix need checking. Life insurers introduce different liability, guarantee and asset-liability considerations. Reinsurance can alter exposures while adding counterparty and contract risks. [S14](../sources.md#s14)

A low reported claims period does not establish that reserves are sufficient. Catastrophe exposure, claims inflation, litigation and changes in underwriting standards can affect outcomes over time. Investment income is relevant but cannot indefinitely compensate for underpriced risk without its own assumptions. When evaluating a put, consider that adverse underwriting news and declining investment assets can arrive together.

## Real estate and REITs

Property businesses require attention to assets, tenants, occupancy, lease terms, capital expenditure and financing. Accounting depreciation can make ordinary earnings multiples less useful for some equity REITs; FFO, AFFO and NAV are commonly used supplementary perspectives. Their definitions and reconciliations matter, and a company's AFFO is not automatically comparable with a peer's. A mortgage REIT has materially different economics from an owner of operating property. [S15](../sources.md#s15)

Analyze lease expiry and debt maturity together. A stable current rent stream can conceal future refinancing exposure or large tenant-improvement requirements. Property values based on capitalization rates are sensitive to both income and required returns; appraisal values are not guaranteed sale proceeds. Dividend size alone does not establish coverage after recurring capital needs.

## Commodity producers and cyclicals

For producers tied to commodity prices, recent margins can be an unreliable estimate of sustainable earning power. Damodaran's commodity materials discuss normalizing across a cycle and the dependence of value on commodity assumptions. The normalization itself is uncertain: old averages may not describe a changed supply structure, cost base or policy regime. [S16](../sources.md#s16)

A low multiple at peak earnings can become a high multiple when earnings normalize. Cost position, reserves or resource life, sustaining capital, hedging, environmental obligations and debt maturities may be decisive. Differentiate an operating hedge from an elimination of equity risk. Commodity hedges may expire, cover only part of production or introduce collateral requirements.

For industrials, transport, autos, semiconductors and other cyclicals, map demand, capacity, inventory, order cancellation and operating leverage. Backlog quality matters: an order subject to cancellation is different from a funded, enforceable commitment. A broad industry classification does not show how a particular company's customers and suppliers behave in a downturn.

## Utilities, consumer, technology and healthcare

For utilities, investigate regulatory treatment, allowed and realized returns, capital plans, funding and physical liabilities. Stable customer demand does not remove financing, construction, weather or political risk. For consumer businesses, distinguish pricing power from temporary price increases, examine unit volumes and channels, and consider whether customer budgets or tastes are shifting.

For technology and communication businesses, relevant evidence can include retention, customer acquisition economics, competitive substitution, product concentration, infrastructure spending and dilution. Recurring revenue does not mean permanent revenue. A high gross margin does not settle the cost of obtaining and serving customers or the durability of demand.

For healthcare, the evidence depends on the activity: patents and pipeline concentration for some drug firms; reimbursement, utilization and costs for providers; regulatory approvals and installed base for device makers; reserve estimates and policy changes for insurers. A binary event can dominate near-term option pricing even when the broader sector is viewed as defensive. Do not use medical speculation as an investment fact.

These latter sector prompts are original research questions, not universal empirical claims about every issuer. Resolve them with issuer filings, applicable regulators and dated industry evidence. Conglomerates may require several lenses; ADRs and foreign issuers add domicile, reporting, currency, governance and depositary-contract questions.

## Consequence for wheel selection

A strike, premium and expiration cannot make sector-specific uncertainties disappear. The analyst should be able to explain why ownership is credible under the adverse scenario likely to coincide with assignment. When that explanation depends on an unresolved sector issue, the expert can request focused work or retain a conditional idea rather than force an endorsement.
