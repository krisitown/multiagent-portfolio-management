# 16 — Professional planning context, ethics, tax and boundaries

DRAFT knowledge. This system is an advisory research tool. It does not become a CFA charterholder, CFP professional, registered adviser, tax specialist or accredited investor by using professional educational material.

## What professional frameworks contribute

CFA Institute's curriculum provides structured analytical education in investments. CFP Board's principal knowledge topics place investment planning alongside cash-flow management, debt, insurance, tax, retirement, estate considerations and psychology. This breadth helps prevent a security-level recommendation from ignoring the purpose of the capital. It is a topic map, not permission to invent missing personal information. [S29](../sources.md#s29)

CFA's suitability standard and CFP Board's standards describe duties for their respective covered professionals and contexts. They can inform habits such as understanding objectives, evaluating portfolio context, identifying conflicts and communicating limitations. Their specific legal or professional applicability must not be generalized to every investor or to this AI. [S30](../sources.md#s30)

“Accredited investor” is a legal eligibility concept in certain jurisdictions and offerings, not a synonym for an experienced investor or a professional qualification. Do not infer it from wealth, trading history or the user's request for sophisticated material. The core wheel research scope does not require making that determination.

## Goals, obligations and risk capacity

The same trade can be reasonable for one capital pool and unsuitable for another. Cash needed for a near-term bill, emergency reserve or known tax payment may not be available to finance an uncertain share purchase. Retirement spending, employment-linked equity, debt and other household exposures can matter if supplied by the owner. Their absence from the snapshot is a limitation, not evidence they do not exist.

A stated desire for income needs interpretation. It may mean regular spendable cash, lower portfolio volatility, a target total return or simply a preference for premium strategies. These are different goals. A material owner question can remain in the ordinary final assessment for a later authorized follow-up. This does not create an interactive waiting tool, Lead reentry or a requirement for a full financial-planning questionnaire.

Leverage can connect investment losses with obligations outside the strategy. The owner's ability to add cash must be evidenced if a proposal relies on it. Never assume a rescue deposit, outside borrowing or sale of unrelated assets. Even a strong long-term thesis may be incompatible with a short funding horizon.

## Taxes and after-tax comparisons

Economic P&L, cash flow, financial reporting and tax recognition can differ. Jurisdiction, tax residence, account type, lot history, holding periods, option characteristics, currency and other transactions can change treatment. US-listed instruments do not establish that the owner is a US taxpayer.

IRS Publication 550 discusses US federal investment-tax topics including options and covered-call complications. It is a US-specific source, dated by tax year, and cannot settle another jurisdiction or an individual's full facts. Use it as a pointer when relevant, not as a universal rule or authority to compute a tax result without sufficient information. [S31](../sources.md#s31)

A net-credit roll may close one economic position at a loss while tax recognition follows separate rules. An effective entry convention is not necessarily statutory basis. Avoid describing all option premium as immediate taxable income, all rolls as tax deferrals or every loss as currently deductible. If tax can materially change the comparison, mark it unresolved and identify the information or professional advice needed.

## Conflicts, communication and privacy

The expert should not favor a source because it sells a strategy or receives a transaction benefit. Source incentives do not automatically invalidate information, but they affect how claims should be checked. Distinguish official contract terms, professional education, issuer claims, analyst opinions and marketing.

Communicate the strongest supported conclusion, including reasons to wait or reject it. A technically accurate table can still mislead if its most consequential limitation is hidden in a footnote. Preserve readiness labels and warnings when summarizing. Do not upgrade an unverified idea through confident language.

Use only authorized portfolio evidence. Do not seek private account data through general links, assume access from a source reference or expose sensitive details in unnecessary examples. Synthetic cases demonstrate concepts without borrowing the owner's personal holdings. Their invented company names, prices and circumstances must remain clearly labeled.

The peripheral topics in this library help recognize when a recommendation depends on wider circumstances. They do not expand the team's mandate into estate planning, insurance sales, private-offering selection or execution. A bounded, useful result can identify a dependency and continue the parts of the investment analysis that remain supportable.
