# Wheel expert — source register

DRAFT v0.2. Access/review date: 2026-10-30. Sources were inspected by the author and/or the named Luna reviewers; the review files record scope and limitations. This register is provenance, not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage, full-book access, institutional endorsement or current account capacity.

References identify support for narrow claims. The surrounding questions, examples and tradeoff analysis are original synthesis, not condensed replacements for the works. Public curriculum summaries are distinguished from restricted full readings. Publisher metadata establishes identity and stated themes, not independent critical consensus or investment performance. All synthetic cases are original and use no private holdings or held-out answer keys.

## Bibliographic and maintenance limits

This is a deliberately selective synthesis of foundational investing ideas, professional education and market mechanics. It cannot represent all human investment knowledge. Sources can be incomplete, dated or internally simplified. Resolve a material live question with the applicable current primary source and supplied account facts through analysts.

No book-derived rule overrides current evidence, owner policy, product terms or role boundaries. A source update requires reviewing affected claims; a changed live fact is a new evidence revision. Draft review does not grant owner approval. Future readiness must be recorded against the exact content the owner approves.
