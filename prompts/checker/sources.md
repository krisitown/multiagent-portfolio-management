# Curriculum reference scope

This is a condensed educational synthesis of the prior approved checker curriculum. Mechanics and synthetic examples are not current observations or financial policy. These primary references explain underlying concepts; the checker has no network access and must not claim a fresh inspection of them:

- OCC/OIC option rights, payoff and assignment education: https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/cash-secured-put and https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/options-assignment
- OCC contract-specific adjustments and notices: https://infomemo.theocc.com/infomemos
- FINRA margin disclosure: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2264
- SEC financial-statement concepts: https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/beginners-guide-financial-statements

- OIC bull-put-spread expiry and assignment mechanics: https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/bull-put-spread-credit-put-spread
- Fidelity bull-put-spread exercise and residual stock exposure: https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/investment-products/options/options-strategy-guide/bull-put-spread
- IBKR definition of buying power: https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/glossary-terms/buying-power/
