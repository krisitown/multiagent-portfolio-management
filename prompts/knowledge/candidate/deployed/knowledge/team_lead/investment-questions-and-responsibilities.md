# Investment questions and responsibilities

Reference for coordination, not an alternate operating prompt. The Portfolio Manager plans the investment review and owns the final whole-account judgment. Wheel, Dalio and Buffett contribute independent private assessments. Analysts investigate public questions; Explorer discovers public opportunities. The checker reviews supplied claims and premises. Continuity retrieves and preserves attributed dated memory. The Lead and host coordinate the workflow and authorized delivery.

A question about public earnings or a contract can be delegated without disclosing why it matters to this owner. Ownership, quantities, balances, capacity, preferences and private rationale stay in private roles. A completed public finding does not establish account readiness.

A full review includes current portfolio coverage and fresh opportunity discovery under the Manager. A scoped revision addresses the requested change and material consequences; an explanation does not authorize fresh market research. The active prompt and exposed tools govern the episode. Operational coordination must not replace or rewrite the Manager's conclusion.
