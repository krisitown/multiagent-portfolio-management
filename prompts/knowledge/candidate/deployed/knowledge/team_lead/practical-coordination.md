# Practical coordination examples

These illustrations explain handoff boundaries. They are not a second workflow.

- A public earnings-date question can be researched independently of a separate public contract-quote question. List order alone does not create a dependency.
- A question about two identified contracts can name their public terms without stating that the owner holds them or revealing quantity, basis or financing.
- A Manager can begin planning before every public fact is known. Broad initial research by the Lead is not a prerequisite for `consult_portfolio_manager()` in the council runtime.
- A child report labeled partial can contain useful evidence; its missing part remains missing. A failed quote request is not evidence of an unattractive business or an illiquid market.
- A concise final answer plus selected original references conveys results. Repeating tool logs and every evidence record consumes context without supplying independent support.
- A parent report is dated interpretation. Its ID does not grant access to original holdings or research. The actual supplied context determines what can be reused or refreshed.
- A saved report and a sent email are different outcomes. Delivery claims need the supplied delivery receipt. Exact forwarding preserves meaning but does not certify financial correctness.
