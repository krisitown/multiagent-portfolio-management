# Analyst source register — draft v0.5

**Status:** Supporting notes for an owner-review draft, not approved production knowledge. Sources were consulted during the 2026-10-30 authoring session through primary-source browsing, with independent Luna cross-checks. Source dates below describe documents or visible updates where recorded; they are not market-data timestamps.

The prompt's inline links identify the supporting material near the relevant factual claims. Analytical lenses and synthetic cases are original teaching/research material, not claims of institutional endorsement or empirical outperformance. No full articles or source PDFs have been copied into this directory. These live URLs are not immutable source snapshots; future source revisions require renewed verification where material.

## Stock ownership and fundamentals

1. [Investor.gov — Stocks](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks): ownership, distributions, and equity risk. Security-specific rights still require the actual issuer/class documents.
2. [Investor.gov — Introduction to Investing](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing): value changes and distributions as return components. No performance promise is imported.
3. [SEC — American Depositary Receipts](https://www.sec.gov/investor/alerts/adr-bulletin.pdf): August 2012 educational bulletin; ratios, depositary structure, fees, and foreign exposure. Current issuer/depositary arrangements must be checked separately.
4. [Investor.gov — How to Read a 10-K/10-Q](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/how-read): filing sections and evidentiary role. The prompt does not copy a historical filing-item checklist or assume SEC approval of an issuer's statements.
5. [SEC — Beginners' Guide to Financial Statements](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/beginners-guide-financial-statements): statement roles and elementary ratio concepts; educational source, not accounting-standard text.
6. [SEC Chief Accountant — Statement of Cash Flows](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/munter-statement-cash-flows-120423): December 4, 2023 staff statement on cash-flow information and reporting judgment.
7. [SEC — Non-GAAP Financial Measures](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/non-gaap-financial-measures): visible last update December 13, 2022; cited sections concern adjustments, FCF limitations, and FFO. Recheck applicable current guidance for a live reporting question.
8. [FASB — Statement 128, Earnings per Share](https://storage.fasb.org/aop_fas128.pdf): historical original-pronouncement text as amended, used only for the basic/diluted conceptual distinction. It is explicitly not presented as the current complete accounting authority.
9. [Federal Reserve — Data Sources and Terms](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2025-december-supervision-and-regulation-report-data-sources-terms.htm): December 2025 reference; used for banking-reporting concepts, not current regulatory thresholds or industry values.
10. [NAIC — Statutory Accounting Principles](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/statutory-accounting-principles): visible update June 23, 2026; statutory reporting's purpose and distinction from GAAP.
11. [NAIC — Risk-Based Capital](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/risk-based-capital): visible update June 30, 2026; RBC's regulatory role and limitations. No capital threshold is adopted as owner policy.
12. [Nareit — FFO White Paper, 2018 Restatement](https://www.reit.com/sites/default/files/2018-FFO-white-paper-%2811-27-18%29.pdf): supplemental performance purpose, limitations, and adjusted-measure comparability. Replaced the older workshop/2002 reference during review.

## Wheel and option mechanics

13. [OIC — June Key Takeaways: Wheel Strategy and Credit Spreads](https://www.optionseducation.org/news/june-key-takeaways-the-wheel-strategy-and-credit-spreads): June 2026; used narrowly for the descriptive wheel sequence. Its generalized strategy commentary is not imported as a mandatory policy or empirical result.
14. [OIC — Cash-Secured Put](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/cash-secured-put): structure and payoff trade-offs. The prompt does not adopt a presumption that assignment is desirable.
15. [OIC — Covered Call](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/covered-call-buy-write): combined-position structure and trade-offs. Stock basis and costs remain explicit in examples.
16. [FINRA — Options](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/options): introductory option rights/obligations and product distinctions. Actual series specifications control.
17. [OIC — Splits, Mergers, Spinoffs and Bankruptcies](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/splits-mergers-spinoffs-bankruptcies): adjustment concepts. No actual adjusted deliverable is inferred without its OCC memo.
18. [OCC — Options Disclosure Document](https://www.theocc.com/company-information/documents-and-archives/options-disclosure-document): document landing page identifies the June 2024 edition. The prompt links the official distribution point and requires current applicable text when dated mechanics matter; the full ODD was not reproduced or exhaustively audited here.
19. [OIC — Options Assignment](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/options-assignment): assignment uncertainty and process. The draft does not import headline aggregate exercise statistics as personal assignment probabilities.
20. [OIC — Exercising Options](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/exercising-options): exercise instructions, assignment, and dividend/expiration considerations. Broker cutoffs remain account-specific facts to verify.
21. [OIC — June Office Hours FAQs](https://www.optionseducation.org/news/june-office-hours-faqs-iv-statistics-position-management-and-the-wheel-strategy): June 2026; IV statistics and after-hours uncertainty. Provider definitions and assumptions remain explicit.
22. [FINRA — Trading Options: Understanding Assignment](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/trading-options-understanding-assignment): December 14, 2020 educational article; used for assignment/obligation concepts. The draft does not generalize its standard 100-share examples to all contracts.
23. [OIC — Options Pricing](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/options-pricing): intrinsic/extrinsic value and pricing inputs; no fixed premium relationship is used to infer executable pricing.
24. [OIC — Delta](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/delta): local sensitivity and position signs.
25. [OIC — Gamma](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/gamma): changing delta and ordinary vanilla-option exposure.
26. [OIC — Theta](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/theta): time sensitivity and model dependence; no income forecast is inferred.
27. [OIC — Vega](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/vega): IV sensitivity and percentage-point units.
28. [OIC — Rho](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/rho): rate sensitivity and model assumptions.
29. [OIC — General Information](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/general-information): open interest, trading activity, liquidity, and their limitations. No listing threshold from the page is adopted as universe policy.

## Portfolio and margin

30. [Investor.gov — Asset Allocation and Diversification](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation): diversification and personal objectives. It does not establish an owner allocation or a numerical correlation model.
31. [FINRA Rule 2264 — Margin Disclosure Statement](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2264): account-margin risk, house requirements, and liquidation rights. No broker capacity is computed from this rule.
32. [Investor.gov — Understanding Margin Accounts](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-29): margin mechanics, risks, and account-specific terms. The draft deliberately avoids encoding changing day-trading thresholds or a universal margin formula.

## Access and version limitations

An FDIC PDF initially considered for bank metrics returned 403 in the browse tool. It is not a retained citation in the final prompt; the bank wording now uses directly inspectable Federal Reserve definitions. No unavailable source is represented as fully inspected. Educational material was used for bounded concepts, not as a substitute for current legal, accounting, contract, issuer, or broker evidence in a live assignment.

The source register is not a market-data refresh or a guarantee of future link availability. Knowledge examples have no live prices, actual account data, current tax determination, or empirical strategy-return claims.

## Volatility interpretation — R21 conceptual support

[OIC, Volatility Skew and Options: An Overview](https://www.optionseducation.org/news/volatility-skew-and-options-an-overview-1), October 2023, supports smile and downside-smirk distinctions. [BIS, Volatility concepts and the risk premium](https://www.bis.org/publications/volatility-concepts-and-risk-premium), September 2014, separates implied volatility from a risk premium relative to expected realized volatility. Accessed 2026-11-02. Neither supplies a current issuer estimate or validates an account trade.
