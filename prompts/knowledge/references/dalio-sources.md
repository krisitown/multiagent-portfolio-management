## Curriculum source register

These bibliographic identifiers resolve the narrow attributed public principles in the curriculum. They are not live evidence receipts or proof that a source has been fetched during this review. For a current claim, inspect relevant current evidence through available tools and use the returned authorized reference. General financial explanations and synthetic cases are original teaching synthesis, not quotations or reconstructions of these works. Historical frameworks are hypotheses to examine, not established laws of market returns.

- D01 — Ray Dalio, [How the Economic Machine Works](https://economicprinciples.org/how-the-economic-machine-works), 2013. Simplified productivity and debt-cycle framework.
- D02 — Ray Dalio, [Principles for Navigating Big Debt Crises](https://www.bridgewater.com/big-debt-crises/principles-for-navigating-big-debt-crises-by-ray-dalio.pdf), 2018. Debt, financing and policy distinctions; a historical framework with transfer limits.
- D03 — Bridgewater, [The All Weather Story](https://www.bridgewater.com/research-and-insights/the-all-weather-story), 2012. Firm retrospective on risk across growth and inflation environments; not a universal retail allocation or a claim of current holdings.
- D04 — Ray Dalio, [How Countries Go Broke: five-minute overview](https://economicprinciples.org/five-minute-read), 2025. Dated debt-policy perspective; its forecasts are not current facts in this curriculum.
- D05 — Ray Dalio, [Believability-weighted idea meritocracy](https://www.principles.com/principles/63fd198e-0b49-4fa9-9571-86b096a7c846/). Decision principle, not evidence that an AI persona has the author's record.
- D06 — Ray Dalio, [Changing World Order charts](https://economicprinciples.org/DalioChangingWorldOrderCharts.pdf). Historical comparative framework; neither a timing model nor proof that national power maps directly to shareholder returns.
- D07 — Ray Dalio, [Remember to reflect when you experience pain](https://www.principles.com/principles/9decb01f-5551-48b9-adf8-667670f4853e/). Learning principle; outcomes still require careful interpretation.

## Further primary references and temporal limits

These are reference locations for product and financing mechanics. They do not establish current rates, eligibility, the owner's legal position or a live account observation. The curriculum's explanations are original general knowledge, and all worked cases are synthetic. Inspect the applicable current product terms and broker evidence when a recommendation depends on them. Bibliographic URLs are not substitutes for exact evidence-store references in tool calls.

- [OCC, Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options](https://www.theocc.com/company-information/documents-and-archives/options-disclosure-document): exercise, settlement and option risks; use the applicable current edition and actual contract specifications.
- [Interactive Brokers, margin requirements](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/margin-requirements.php) and [margin rates](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/margin-rates.php): broker terms and dated financing schedules, not permanent promises of account capacity.
- [SEC Investor.gov, bonds](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/bonds-or-fixed-income-products/bonds) and [bond funds](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/bond-funds-and-income-funds): basic distinctions between individual contractual claims and continuing fund portfolios. Educational material does not replace product documents or establish suitability.
